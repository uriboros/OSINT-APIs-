# OSINT-APIs-
This repository is a curated collection of Open Source Intelligence (OSINT), Privacy, and Operational Security (OPSEC) resources gathered over many years. Originally maintained as private bookmarks and research notes, these materials have been organized and published to make them more accessible to the wider community.

The goal of this project is to provide a centralized reference for tools, techniques, and educational resources used in OSINT investigations, privacy research, and operational security practices.

This repository is continuously maintained and evolving. New resources are added regularly as they are discovered, while outdated or inactive entries are periodically reviewed.

Scope

The repository includes links to a wide range of resources, including:

OSINT tools and investigative utilities

Privacy and anonymity resources

OPSEC practices and methodologies

Research techniques and tradecraft

Educational materials, blogs, and documentation

Open-source software and GitHub projects

While OPSEC and Privacy are distinct disciplines, there is often overlap in the tools and practices used. For this reason, relevant privacy-related resources are included where appropriate.

Resource Availability

Due to the dynamic nature of OSINT and privacy technologies:

Some resources may become outdated or unavailable over time

Certain tools may require payment or subscription, although some may offer free tiers or trials

Technical difficulty varies from beginner-friendly tools to advanced research utilities

Many of the projects listed are maintained by independent developers and researchers contributing in their spare time.

If you encounter broken links or outdated content, contributions and notifications are appreciated so the repository can remain accurate and useful.

Disclaimer

Links to external websites, tools, blogs, or other resources do not constitute endorsement or recommendation. This repository has no commercial affiliations or partnerships with any listed services.

Due to the large number of resources included, not every tool has been personally tested. Users should independently evaluate any resource before use and understand the potential implications for their privacy, data security, and investigative footprint.

OSINT Categories Used by Intelligence Teams
1. Identity Intelligence (SOCMINT / HUMINT Support)

Tools used to identify individuals and link identities.

Examples:

People search

Username enumeration

Email investigation

Phone number intelligence

Identity correlation

Typical use cases:

Identifying suspects

Linking aliases

Social media attribution

2. Social Media Intelligence (SOCMINT)

Collection and analysis of social media data.

Focus areas:

Account analysis

Network mapping

Post analysis

Influence tracking

Trend monitoring

Platforms often investigated:

Twitter / X

Facebook

Instagram

TikTok

LinkedIn

Telegram

Reddit

3. Domain & Infrastructure Intelligence

Investigation of websites, domains, and internet infrastructure.

Includes:

WHOIS records

DNS records

Subdomain discovery

Website technology stack

SSL certificate intelligence

Commonly used in:

Cyber threat intelligence

Fraud investigations

Attribution research

4. Network Intelligence

Investigation of IP addresses, servers, and network infrastructure.

Examples:

IP geolocation

Open port scanning

Device fingerprinting

Internet exposure analysis

Common tools:

Shodan

Censys

FOFA

ZoomEye

5. Image Intelligence (IMINT)

Analysis of images for investigative leads.

Techniques:

Reverse image search

Metadata extraction

Image manipulation detection

Facial recognition (where legally allowed)

6. Geospatial Intelligence (GEOINT)

Location analysis using geographic data.

Includes:

Satellite imagery

Geolocation from photos

Terrain analysis

Building identification

Event verification

Common tools:

Google Earth

Sentinel Hub

Mapillary

OpenStreetMap

7. Video Intelligence

Investigation and verification of video content.

Focus:

Frame analysis

Geolocation of footage

Timeline verification

Audio clues

Used heavily in:

War investigations

Disinformation analysis

Crisis reporting

8. Corporate Intelligence

Investigation of businesses, organizations, and ownership structures.

Examples:

Corporate registries

Company ownership

Financial filings

Offshore companies

Supply chain analysis

9. Dark Web Intelligence

Investigation of hidden services and underground marketplaces.

Includes:

Darknet monitoring

Threat actor activity

Criminal forums

Investigation of exposed datasets and breached information.

Includes:

Breach databases

11. Financial Intelligence

Tracking financial flows and assets.

Cryptocurrency tracking

Blockchain analysis

Fraud detection

12. Threat Intelligence

Used in cybersecurity investigations.

Includes:

Malware infrastructure

Investigation of files and documents.

Techniques:

Metadata extraction

14. Transportation Intelligence

Tracking movement of vehicles, ships, and aircraft.

Examples:

Flight tracking

15. Archive Intelligence

Deleted websites
